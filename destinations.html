Top Places to Visit in Greece

Explore the best islands, historic cities, and scenic coastal spots across Hellas.

Cyclades Islands

Santorini

White-washed towns & volcanic sunsets

Famous for iconic blue-domed churches in Oia, breathtaking caldera views, and unique black and red sand beaches.

Capital & History

Athens

The historic heart of philosophy & ancient ruins

Visit the magnificent Parthenon on the Acropolis hill, stroll through the vibrant Plaka neighborhood, and sample world-class cuisine.

Ionian Islands

Zakynthos

Home of the famous Shipwreck Beach

Renowned for the legendary Navagio Cove, electric-blue sea caves, and golden coastlines where sea turtles nest.

Cyclades Islands

Mykonos

Charming windmills & vibrant coastal life

Explore narrow stone alleyways in Little Venice, famous historic windmills, and picturesque beach lounges.

Southern Aegean

Crete

Pink beaches & ancient Minoan palaces

Greece’s largest island features the famous Elafonisi pink sand lagoon, Venetian harbors in Chania, and the Knossos Palace.

Mainland Greece

Meteora

Monasteries perched atop towering rock pillars

A surreal UNESCO heritage site featuring ancient monasteries balance atop gigantic sandstone cliffs in central Greece.
